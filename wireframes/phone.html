Numbered UX & HCI Annotations: Phone Call Questionnaire

  • 1

    Miller's Law (Information Chunking)

    The 10 questions are structured into two distinct 5-question cognitive chunks (Part 1: Demands & Passwords; Part 2: Impersonation & Panic Threats). This honors the 7 ± 2 working memory threshold and avoids overwhelming anxious victims.

    Checklist 3: Miller's Law (Chunking)
  • 2

    Visibility of System Status (Nielsen #1)

    Dynamic live progress bar recalculates progress percentage (0% to 100%) and updates the count indicator in real-time as questions are answered, giving users immediate task closure feedback.

    Checklist 2: Nielsen #1 Visibility of System Status
  • 3

    Symmetrical Binary Selection (WCAG 2.2)

    Each question uses large, touch-friendly binary cards for "🛡️ No" and "⚠️ Yes" with clear high-contrast active states. Fulfills the >44px touch target requirement for shaky or senior hands.

    Checklist 9: Accessible Forms & Touch Targets
  • 4

    Plain-Language Red Flag Indicators

    Mentions common real-world application names ("AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport") and specific tactics (electricity cut, courier parcel, police arrest) rather than abstract cybersecurity jargon.

    Checklist 2: Nielsen #2 Match Real World
