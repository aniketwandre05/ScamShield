Numbered UX & HCI Annotations: Check Email Phishing

  • 1

    Progressive Form Disclosure & Form Usability

    Users only need to provide the email body (marked with *). Sender and Subject fields are explicitly flagged as (Optional), reducing cognitive barriers for hasty or stressed users.

    Checklist 2: Nielsen #8 Minimalist Design
  • 2

    Multi-Field Atomic Reset Helper

    The "Clear Form" button uses data attributes (data-target, data-target-extra, data-target-sender) to clear all three inputs atomically with a single tap, reducing repetitive manual backspacing.

    Checklist 3: Shneiderman #6 Easy Reversal
  • 3

    Composite Scenario Demonstration

    Demo buttons populate all three fields simultaneously with realistic spoofed sender headers, urgent subject lines, and deceptive links, demonstrating real-world spear-phishing mechanics.

    Checklist 2: Nielsen #6 Recognition over Recall
