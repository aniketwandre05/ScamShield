Numbered UX & HCI Annotations: Scan History (Populated & Empty)

  • 1

    Transparent Data Ephemerality

    Header clearly states that records are stored purely inside active browser session memory and are never persisted to a remote relational database, satisfying privacy compliance and user trust needs.

    Checklist 8: Trust, Safety & Ethics
  • 2

    Real-Time Client-Side Filter & Search

    Enables instant multi-criteria sorting (filtering by Risk Level and instant substring search across message text). Reduces cognitive load for users inspecting multiple suspicious items.

    Checklist 4: Search & Filter Information Architecture
  • 3

    Error Prevention on Destructive Actions

    Clicking "Clear History" does not blindly delete data; it invokes a modal dialog (with Escape key support and keyboard focus trap) requiring explicit confirmation before clearing session items.

    Checklist 2: Nielsen #5 Error Prevention
  • 4

    Institutional Export (CSV & Print)

    Users who need to report extortion or financial fraud to banks or law enforcement can export clean structured CSV records or print a formal audit log with a single click.

    Checklist 6: Interaction Design & Export
  • 5

    Empathetic Empty State Guidance

    When no scans have occurred (or after history is cleared), the screen displays an illustrated empty mailbox state with positive guidance directing users back to the home dashboard.

    Checklist 2: Nielsen #9 Recover from Empty States
