Numbered UX & HCI Annotations: Check SMS / WhatsApp

  • 1

    Easy Reversal & Breadcrumb Fallback

    Includes an unambiguous top link ("← Home / Choose Another Check") supporting Shneiderman's Rule #6 (Easy Reversal of Actions), letting users back out gracefully without browser reloads.

    Checklist 3: Shneiderman #6 Easy Reversal
  • 2

    Dual-Mode Input Tabs (Norman's Signifiers)

    Explicit visual tabs differentiate text pasting from screenshot uploading. Users can either type/paste directly or drop an image for optical character recognition (OCR).

    Checklist 6: Interaction Design & Multimodal Input
  • 3

    Pre-emptive Privacy Calibration

    Transparent notice reassuring users that passwords, OTPs, and credit card numbers are automatically sanitized and masked by regex privacy filters before server evaluation.

    Checklist 8: Trust, Safety & Ethics
  • 4

    Recognition Over Recall (Demo Examples)

    Provides pre-configured safe and fraudulent test scenarios (e.g. Electricity Bill Disconnection, KYC Block). One click populates the input, enabling immediate evaluation without having to copy-paste live scams.

    Checklist 2: Nielsen #6 Recognition over Recall
  • 5

    Clipboard Efficiency Tools

    Dedicated "Paste" and "Clear" helper buttons sitting directly above the textarea facilitate single-tap operation on mobile and tablet devices (WCAG 2.2 touch-target compliance).

    Checklist 2: Nielsen #7 Flexibility & Efficiency
