Numbered UX & HCI Annotations: Error Handling & Recovery

  • 1

    Plain-Language Diagnostic Messages

    ScamShield rejects raw HTTP codes (e.g. 400 Bad Request, 500 Internal Error) and replaces them with user-centered, polite explanations ("Could Not Read Screenshot" / "The image was blurry...").

    Checklist 2: Nielsen #9 Help Users Recover
  • 2

    Direct Reversal Return Paths

    Provides explicit primary action ("🔄 Try Again") pointing dynamically back to the calling checker URL, alongside a secondary route ("🏠 Back to Home") to prevent dead-end traps.

    Checklist 3: Shneiderman #6 Easy Reversal
  • 3

    Error Recovery Jump Panel

    Renders 4 immediate shortcut chips directly to the other detection channels (SMS, Email, URL, Phone), allowing users to switch modalities smoothly without restarting navigation.

    Checklist 4: Navigation Architecture
