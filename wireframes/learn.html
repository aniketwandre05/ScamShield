Scam Awareness & Learning Center

Learn how scammers manipulate victims using psychological urgency, recognize common fraud patterns, and test your skills.

🚨 Common Scam Tactics & Red Flags

1 COMMON TRAP

⚡ Electricity Bill Disconnection

The Trick: You receive an SMS claiming power will be cut at 9:30 PM due to an unpaid bill, urging a call to a personal number.

🚩 Red Flags: Same-night deadline panic, unofficial 10-digit mobile number, demands app installation.
PHISHING

🏦 Bank KYC / PAN Card Block

The Trick: SMS claiming your bank account or debit card has been frozen due to expired KYC documents or missing PAN card.

🚩 Red Flags: Shortened URL (bit.ly/tinyurl), requests net banking credentials or OTP on fake login forms.
HIGH LOSS

🖥️ Remote Screen Sharing Trap

The Trick: Caller poses as customer support or telecom officer helping you update 5G SIM by installing a remote access utility.

🚩 Red Flags: Requests a 9-digit AnyDesk code, allowing criminals to view incoming banking OTPs directly on screen.
LURE TRAP

🎁 Lottery / Work From Home

The Trick: Claims you won a cash lottery or promises ₹5,000/day for liking YouTube videos or reviewing hotels on Telegram.

🚩 Red Flags: Demands an upfront "processing deposit" or "registration fee" before releasing winnings.

🧠 Test Your Scam Detection Instincts

Scenario 2: Caller claiming to be from your bank asks for the 6-digit OTP sent to your phone to reverse an accidental debit. Share it?
✅ Correct! Banks never demand one-time verification passcodes over voice calls.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

3
What should I do if I already clicked a suspicious link?
1. Do not enter any passwords, OTPs, or credit card numbers on the opened page.
2. Close the browser tab immediately.
3. If credentials were submitted, change them immediately from a safe device and enable 2-factor authentication.
4. Freeze bank cards via your mobile banking app if financial details were exposed.
Why does ScamShield say assessments are estimates rather than 100% guarantees?
In accordance with HCI trust calibration principles, no automated system can guarantee 100% accuracy against unseen novel attack variants. ScamShield transparently highlights fraud markers and empowers you with safe decision-making steps.
How do I file an official police complaint in India?
4 Dial 1930 immediately to freeze defrauded money in transit, and file an incident report at cybercrime.gov.in.
