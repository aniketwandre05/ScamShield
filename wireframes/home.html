Numbered UX & HCI Annotations: Home Dashboard

  • 1

    Senior & Low-Vision Inclusivity Toolbar

    Header provides immediate accessibility controls: A- / A / A+ font zoom factors (0.88x to 1.35x), dark/light mode toggle, high-contrast, dyslexia font, and instant language switching (EN, MR, HI). Adheres to ISO 9241-110 Individualisation.

    Checklist 9: WCAG 2.2 & Inclusivity
  • 2

    Choice Minimization & Fitts's Law

    Instead of overwhelming users with menus, the dashboard presents 4 prominent cards representing the primary threat channels (SMS, Email, URL/QR, Phone). Large targets (>44px) minimize motor effort and decision fatigue.

    Checklist 3: Hick-Hyman & Fitts's Law
  • 3

    Direct Keyboard Shortcuts

    Each card displays an explicit keyboard shortcut pill ([1], [2], [3], [4]). Keypresses immediately route to the respective checker without requiring mouse clicks, aiding motor-impaired and power users.

    Checklist 3: Shneiderman Rule #2 Shortcuts
  • 4

    First-Time Onboarding & User Control

    A high-contrast 3-step banner guides first-time visitors on how ScamShield works. Includes a distinct dismiss button that persists state in localStorage, preventing repetitive clutter.

    Checklist 12: Onboarding & User Control
