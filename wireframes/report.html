Report a Scam & Official Helplines

Official emergency helplines and quick incident submission to safeguard your community and recover compromised accounts.

1 EMERGENCY HELPLINE

📞 Dial 1930

Dial 1930 immediately if you suffered financial fraud within the last 24 hours to freeze transferred money in transit.

2 NATIONAL CYBERCRIME PORTAL

🌐 cybercrime.gov.in

Official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal operated by Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India.

3

Immediate Checklist If Money or Passwords Were Shared:

1. Freeze Cards & Net Banking: Log into bank app or call official branch helpline to lock ATM debit cards and freeze UPI payments.
2. Call 1930 (Golden Hour): Reporting within 2 to 4 hours drastically increases chances of cyber police freezing destination accounts.
3. Reset Credentials: Change email and net banking passwords from a separate clean phone or computer.
4. Preserve Digital Evidence: Take clear screenshots of SMS, WhatsApp chats, call history, and transaction UPI reference IDs.
