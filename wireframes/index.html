ScamShield Low-Fidelity Design System Specifications

Constructed in accordance with the ScamShield HCI Implementation Checklist and ISO 9241 ergonomics standards.

Design Variable Low-Fidelity Wireframe Specification HCI Checklist Reference & Rationales
Palette Greyscale (White #fff, Greys #f3f4f6, #e5e7eb, #9ca3af, Dark #111827) Prevents aesthetic bias; focuses feedback purely on information architecture, cognitive hierarchy, and affordances.
Typography System sans-serif stack; Minimum 16px body font; 24-28px page headings WCAG 2.2 Guideline 1.4 (Distinguishable text); senior-friendly readability without eye strain.
Touch Targets Minimum 44px × 44px for all buttons, inputs, pills, and cards WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 2.5.8 (Target Size); Fitts's Law optimization for mobile thumb operation.
Risk Encoding Solid Border (Low), Dashed Border (Medium), Double Border (High) + Text Badges WCAG 2.2 Guideline 1.4.1 (Non-color-alone); fully accessible to color-blind and low-vision users.
Iconography Deliberate bounding boxes labeled [icon: Name] Clear low-fidelity signifiers showing placement and semantic meaning without distracting illustrations.
Viewports Side-by-side Mobile (390px) & Desktop (1280px) Responsive parity verification across modern smartphone viewports and wide desktop displays.
