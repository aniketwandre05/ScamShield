Numbered UX & HCI Annotations: Overlays, Modals & Microinteractions

  • 1

    Visibility of System Status (Analyzing Loader)

    Triggered on form submission to prevent multiple clicks and provide instant feedback while ML classifiers and OCR run. Explains system status in plain language ("Inspecting text patterns...").

    Checklist 2: Nielsen #1 Visibility of System Status
  • 2

    Accessible Confirmation Modal (Error Prevention)

    Protects destructive actions (clearing history). Traps keyboard focus inside the dialog, responds to the Escape key, and sets focus to the Cancel button by default to prevent accidental deletion.

    Checklist 2: Nielsen #5 Error Prevention
  • 3

    Toast Notification Microinteractions

    Non-blocking floating toast alerts appear in the lower-right corner to acknowledge background events (copying summary, resetting inputs, changing fonts) without interrupting reading flow.

    Checklist 6: Microinteractions & Snackbars
  • 4

    Inclusivity Dropdown Menu

    Houses specialized accessibility settings (OpenDyslexic typeface and high-contrast CSS overrides) in an accessible toggled dropdown menu with full aria-expanded semantics.

    Checklist 9: WCAG 2.2 Accessibility Options
