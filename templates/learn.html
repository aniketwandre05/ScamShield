Common Trap

⚡ Electricity Bill Disconnection

The Trick: You receive an SMS claiming your power will be cut tonight at 9:30 PM due to an unpaid bill, urging you to call an unknown officer.

🚩 Red Flags: Urgent deadline (same night), unofficial personal mobile number, demands immediate payment or app install.
Phishing

🏦 Bank Account KYC / PAN Block

The Trick: Messages stating your bank account or debit card has been frozen due to expired KYC or missing PAN card.

🚩 Red Flags: Shortened URL (bit.ly/tinyurl), requests your net banking credentials or SMS OTP on a fake login page.
High Loss

🖥️ Remote Screen Share (AnyDesk / TeamViewer)

The Trick: Caller poses as customer care or telecom officer claiming to help you update SIM or refund money by installing a remote access tool.

🚩 Red Flags: Asks you to share a 9-digit code, letting them see your screen and read incoming OTPs to drain accounts.
Lure Trap

🎁 Lottery / Giveaway / Work From Home

The Trick: Claims you won a lottery or promises ₹5,000/day for liking YouTube videos or rating hotels on Telegram.

🚩 Red Flags: Asks for a "registration fee" or "processing deposit" before releasing your supposed winnings.

❓ {{ t('learn_faq_title') }}

What should I do if I already clicked a suspicious link?

1. Do not enter any passwords, OTPs, or card numbers on the webpage.

2. Close the browser tab immediately.

3. If you submitted passwords, change them immediately from a secure device and enable 2-factor authentication.

4. Check your bank statement and freeze your cards if financial details were exposed.

Why does ScamShield say an assessment is an estimate rather than 100% guarantee?

In accordance with HCI trust calibration principles, no automated system can guarantee 100% accuracy against brand-new, unseen scam variants. ScamShield transparently highlights fraud markers and empowers you with safe decision-making steps.

Is my scanned text or screenshot kept on your servers?

No. ScamShield processes screenshots in memory and temporary files are wiped immediately upon OCR completion. Your scan history is stored exclusively inside your active browser session.

Where can I report financial cyber fraud in India?

Call the National Cyber Crime Helpline at 1930 immediately within the "Golden Hour" (first 2-24 hours). You can also file a complaint online at cybercrime.gov.in.
